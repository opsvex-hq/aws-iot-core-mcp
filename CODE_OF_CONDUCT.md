# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be decent. Assume the person on the other side is
competent and busy.

## Expected

- Technical criticism of code, decisions and documentation — including ours.
  A bug report that says "this is wrong and here is why" is welcome.
- Reporting what you actually observed, rather than what you assume happened.
- Accepting a "no" on scope. Some requests are declined by design, and the
  reasons are written down in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the ADRs.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or dismissing someone rather than their argument.
- Posting a vulnerability publicly instead of following `SECURITY.md`.
- Posting someone else's fleet data, credentials, or customer information in an
  issue. Redact before you paste: thing names and site attributes often identify
  a real customer.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **hola@opsvex.com**. The maintainers will read it, respond
within 5 business days, and act — editing or deleting content, or blocking an
account — as needed. We will keep a report confidential to the extent we can
while acting on it.

This project is maintained by two people. Enforcement is proportionate and
pragmatic, not procedural.
